What is an EC?

An Encumbrance Certificate is a property document that shows all recorded transactions on a property over a specific period. It is commonly used to verify whether a property is free from legal or financial liabilities before a purchase.

  • Usually taken for the last 13 to 30 years, depending on what your bank or buyer asks for
  • Needed for most home loans and before you pay any advance on a property
  • To start, keep the survey number, property address and owner name ready

What is an RTC?

RTC stands for Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops. It is a key land and revenue record associated with agricultural land in Karnataka. The document includes ownership rights, tenancy details and crop-related information and is often required for land transactions.

  • Applies mainly to agricultural land and is also called Pahani
  • Used to confirm who the recorded holder of the land is before a sale
  • To start, keep the survey number, hobli and village details ready
Enquire about RTC records
Useful Guide

What is a Sale Deed?

A Sale Deed is a legal document that records the transfer of property ownership from the seller to the buyer. It is executed at the time of the final property transaction and must be registered at the Sub-Registrar's office. It is the primary proof of property ownership.

  • Registered at the Sub-Registrar office, with stamp duty and registration charges payable
  • Both parties need to be present with ID proof and two witnesses
  • Usually preceded by an Agreement to Sale and a clear EC check
